# Workplace, Hybrid Work and Conduct Policy - Shore Staff
Seacrest Ship Management Pvt Ltd (fictional) - Document HR-POL-05 - Version 2.1 - Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Working Hours
The standard working week is Monday to Friday, 40 hours per week. Core hours, during which all employees must be available, are 10:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time. The fleet operations desk works in rotating shifts and follows the separate shift roster.

## 2. Hybrid Work
Eligible employees may work from home up to 2 days per week after completing their probation period. Hybrid work days must be agreed with the reporting manager and recorded in the HRMS attendance module by Friday of the previous week. Employees in the fleet operations desk, the crewing front office and the reception are not eligible for hybrid work.
Employees working from home receive an internet reimbursement of INR 1,000 per month. The company provides a laptop and headset; other home-office equipment is not reimbursed.

## 3. Information Security when Working Remotely
Company data may be accessed only on company-issued laptops connected through the corporate VPN. Vessel and crew personal data must never be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 24 hours.

## 4. Probation and Confirmation
New employees serve a probation period of 6 months. The probation may be extended once by up to 3 months. Confirmation is communicated in writing by HR.

## 5. Notice Period
During probation, either party may terminate employment with 15 days notice. After confirmation, the notice period is 60 days for grades E1 to E3 and 90 days for grades M1 and above. Notice period may be bought out with the approval of the HR Head.

## 6. Code of Conduct
Employees must act with integrity, avoid conflicts of interest and comply with anti-bribery rules. Gifts from suppliers, ship owners or agents above INR 3,000 in value must be declared to the Compliance Officer within 7 days and may not be accepted in cash.
Harassment of any kind is prohibited. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee constituted under the POSH Act, which completes its inquiry within 90 days.

## 7. Grievance Redressal
Employees should first raise a grievance with their reporting manager. If unresolved within 10 working days, the grievance may be escalated in writing to the HR Head. Concerns about unethical behaviour can be reported anonymously through the ethics hotline at ethics@seacrest.example.

## 8. Learning and Development
Each confirmed employee may attend up to 5 days of approved external training per year. Course fees above INR 50,000 require a service bond of 12 months.
