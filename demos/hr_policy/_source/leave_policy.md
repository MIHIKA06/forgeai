# Leave Policy - Shore Staff
Seacrest Ship Management Pvt Ltd (fictional) - Document HR-POL-03 - Version 4.2 - Effective 1 April 2026

## 1. Purpose and Applicability
This policy sets out the leave entitlements of permanent shore-based employees working in the Mumbai and Chennai offices. It does not apply to seafarers serving on board vessels, whose leave is governed by their Collective Bargaining Agreement, or to contract staff engaged through agencies.

## 2. Leave Year
The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Entitlements for employees who join or leave during the year are pro-rated on a monthly basis.

## 3. Annual Leave
Every permanent employee is entitled to 24 working days of annual leave per leave year. Annual leave accrues at the rate of 2 days for each completed month of service. Employees on probation accrue annual leave from their date of joining but may only take a maximum of 5 days of annual leave during the six-month probation period.
Annual leave must be applied for through the HRMS portal at least 7 calendar days in advance for absences of up to 3 days, and at least 21 calendar days in advance for absences longer than 3 days. Leave is approved by the reporting manager. During the dry-docking season (October to January) the Technical department may restrict leave of more than 5 consecutive days.

## 4. Carry Forward and Encashment
A maximum of 10 days of unused annual leave may be carried forward into the next leave year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 March unless the HR Head approves an exception in writing because leave was refused for business reasons.
Employees may encash up to 5 days of unused annual leave once per leave year, in the March payroll. Encashment is calculated on basic salary only. On resignation or retirement, the full accrued annual leave balance is encashed in the final settlement.

## 5. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per leave year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be encashed. A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave taken immediately before or after a public holiday also requires a medical certificate.
Employees must inform their manager of sick leave before 10:30 on the first day of absence.

## 6. Casual Leave
Employees receive 6 days of casual leave per leave year for urgent personal matters. Casual leave may not be combined with annual leave and cannot exceed 2 days at a time.

## 7. Parental Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children, and 12 weeks for the third and subsequent children, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. Adoptive mothers adopting a child below three months of age receive 12 weeks of leave.
Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the birth or adoption of the child.

## 8. Bereavement Leave
Employees may take 5 working days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law).

## 9. Public Holidays
Each office observes 12 public holidays per calendar year. The holiday list is published by HR in December for the following year. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive a compensatory day off, to be taken within 60 days.

## 10. Leave Without Pay
Leave without pay of up to 30 days may be granted by the HR Head when all other leave balances are exhausted. Leave without pay longer than 30 days requires approval of the Managing Director.

## 11. Unauthorised Absence
Absence without approved leave for more than 3 consecutive working days is treated as unauthorised absence and may lead to disciplinary action under the Code of Conduct.
