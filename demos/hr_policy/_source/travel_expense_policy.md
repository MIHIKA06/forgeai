# Travel and Expense Policy - Shore Staff
Seacrest Ship Management Pvt Ltd (fictional) - Document FIN-POL-07 - Version 3.0 - Effective 1 January 2026

## 1. Scope
This policy covers business travel and reimbursable expenses for shore-based employees, including superintendents attending vessels in port. All travel must be pre-approved through the Travel Request form in the HRMS portal.

## 2. Approval
Domestic travel is approved by the reporting manager. International travel requires approval by the department head and the Chief Financial Officer. Travel requests must be raised at least 5 working days before departure, except for emergency vessel attendance, which may be approved by phone and regularised within 2 working days.

## 3. Air Travel
Employees travel in economy class on all flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights of 6 hours or longer for employees of Grade M3 and above. Tickets are booked only through the company travel desk; personally booked tickets are not reimbursed unless booked during an emergency vessel attendance.

## 4. Hotel Accommodation
Hotel accommodation is booked through the travel desk within the following nightly limits including taxes:
- Grade E1 to E3: up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and INR 4,000 in other cities.
- Grade M1 to M3: up to INR 9,000 per night in metro cities and INR 6,500 in other cities.
- International travel: up to USD 180 per night for all grades.
Stays exceeding the limit require prior written approval from the department head.

## 5. Daily Allowance (Per Diem)
A daily allowance covers meals and incidental expenses and is paid without receipts:
- Domestic travel: INR 1,500 per day for grades E1 to E3 and INR 2,500 per day for grades M1 to M3.
- International travel: USD 60 per day for all grades.
- Vessel attendance allowance: superintendents attending a vessel in port receive an additional INR 1,200 per day of attendance.
Where meals are provided by the host or the vessel, the daily allowance is reduced by 50 percent.

## 6. Local Conveyance
Taxi and app-based cab fares for business purposes are reimbursed at actuals. Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at INR 12 per kilometre, and a personal two-wheeler at INR 6 per kilometre.

## 7. Expense Claims
Expense claims must be submitted through the HRMS portal within 30 days of returning from travel. Claims submitted after 30 days require department head approval, and claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed. Original or digital receipts are mandatory for every individual expense above INR 500. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll.

## 8. Travel Advances
Employees may request a travel advance of up to 75 percent of the estimated trip cost. Advances must be settled within 15 days of return; unsettled advances are recovered from salary.

## 9. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The company does not reimburse alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, laundry for trips shorter than 4 nights, or upgrades purchased by the employee.
