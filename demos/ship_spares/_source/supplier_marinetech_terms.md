# Supply Terms - Marinetech Spares Pte Ltd (fictional)
Framework agreement with Seacrest Ship Management - Ref MT-FA-2026 - Valid 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2027

## 1. Product Range
Marinetech supplies OEM-certified spares for auxiliary machinery: separators, compressors, pumps, boilers and deck machinery. Marinetech does not supply main engine parts.

## 2. Prices and Discounts
Prices are as per the catalogue in US dollars, ex-warehouse Singapore. Volume discounts are applied per purchase order on the total order value:
- 5 percent on orders above USD 5,000.
- 8 percent on orders above USD 15,000.
Discounts are not applied to freight, packing or surcharges.

## 3. Payment Terms
Payment is due 45 days from the invoice date. Invoices paid within 10 days receive an additional 2 percent early-payment discount.

## 4. Delivery
Standard delivery from the Singapore warehouse to the Singapore or Rotterdam consolidation warehouse is included in the price. Delivery to any other port is charged at cost plus 10 percent. Air freight, when requested, carries a surcharge of 12 percent of the order value.
Lead times in the catalogue are counted in calendar days from purchase order confirmation to dispatch.

## 5. Warranty
All parts carry a warranty of 12 months from the date of delivery or 6 months from installation, whichever comes first. Warranty claims must include the part serial number and a failure report.

## 6. Returns
Unused parts in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery. A restocking fee of 15 percent of the part value applies. Parts specially ordered from the manufacturer cannot be returned.

Orders: orders@marinetech.example. Technical queries: tech@marinetech.example.
