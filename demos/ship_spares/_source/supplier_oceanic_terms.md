# Supply Terms - Oceanic Engine Parts BV (fictional)
Framework agreement with Seacrest Ship Management - Ref OEP-FA-26 - Valid 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2028

## 1. Product Range
Oceanic Engine Parts is an authorised distributor of OEM spares for two-stroke and four-stroke main and auxiliary engines, turbochargers and governors. All parts are supplied with the manufacturer's certificate of conformity.

## 2. Prices and Discounts
Prices are as per the catalogue in US dollars, ex-works Rotterdam. A discount of 4 percent applies to orders above USD 10,000. There is no discount on turbocharger parts.

## 3. Payment Terms
Payment is due 30 days from the invoice date. Late payments attract interest of 1 percent per month.

## 4. Delivery and Urgent Dispatch
Delivery to the Rotterdam consolidation warehouse is free of charge. Delivery to other ports is quoted per order. For criticality A items, Oceanic offers urgent dispatch within 48 hours of order confirmation, for a surcharge of 20 percent of the order value.
Lead times in the catalogue are calendar days from order confirmation to dispatch.

## 5. Warranty
Parts carry an 18-month warranty from the date of delivery. Warranty does not cover wear parts such as gaskets, O-rings and filters.

## 6. Returns
Returns are accepted within 14 days of delivery with a restocking fee of 20 percent. Electrical and electronic parts cannot be returned once the seal is broken.
