# Spare Parts Procurement Policy - Technical Department
Seacrest Ship Management Pvt Ltd (fictional) - Document TEC-PRC-02 - Version 5.0 - Effective 1 July 2026

## 1. Purpose
This policy governs the purchase of machinery spare parts for vessels under technical management. It applies to chief engineers, technical superintendents, fleet managers and the purchase team.

## 2. Requisitions
Every purchase starts with a requisition raised by the chief engineer in the planned maintenance system. A requisition must state the part number, equipment, maker, quantity, required-by date and the port of delivery. Requisitions without a maker part number are returned to the vessel.

## 3. Approval Limits
Purchase orders are approved according to the total order value in US dollars:
- Up to USD 2,000: chief engineer approval, confirmed by the purchase team.
- Above USD 2,000 and up to USD 10,000: technical superintendent approval.
- Above USD 10,000 and up to USD 50,000: fleet manager approval and at least 3 competitive quotations.
- Above USD 50,000: technical director approval, 3 quotations and a written technical justification.
Orders must not be split to stay below an approval limit.

## 4. Critical Spares
Spares classified as criticality A (main engine, steering gear, emergency generator, fire pumps and lifesaving appliances) must be original equipment manufacturer (OEM) or OEM-certified parts. Non-OEM alternatives for criticality A spares are not permitted. For criticality B and C spares, approved non-OEM alternatives may be used if the price is at least 15 percent lower than the OEM offer.

## 5. Minimum Onboard Stock
Each vessel must hold the minimum onboard stock defined in its critical spares list. When onboard stock of a criticality A item falls below the minimum, the chief engineer must raise a requisition within 24 hours.

## 6. Emergency Purchases
An emergency purchase is one needed to prevent off-hire or to restore class or flag compliance. Emergency orders may be placed by the technical superintendent by phone or email and must be regularised in the system within 2 working days. Air freight is permitted only for emergency purchases or criticality A items with a required-by date less than 10 days away.

## 7. Delivery and Receipt
Spares are delivered to the vessel through the agent at the port of delivery or through the Singapore and Rotterdam consolidation warehouses. The chief engineer must confirm receipt in the system within 3 days of delivery and report any damage or shortage within the same period.

## 8. Supplier Selection
Only suppliers on the approved supplier list may be used. Supplier performance is reviewed every 6 months on price, on-time delivery and quality. A supplier with on-time delivery below 85 percent over two consecutive reviews is placed on probation.
