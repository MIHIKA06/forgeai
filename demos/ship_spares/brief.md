Build an assistant for our chief engineers and technical superintendents to help with ship spare-parts procurement. They should be able to look up parts in the spares catalogue (price, lead time, maker, supplier, minimum order quantity), estimate the total cost of an order including supplier discounts, compare supplier terms like warranty, payment and returns, and check which approval level and procurement rules apply. It would be great if it could also show live stock levels in the suppliers' warehouses and track the status of orders we have already placed. Answers should be short and practical - these are engineers, often on board with slow internet.
